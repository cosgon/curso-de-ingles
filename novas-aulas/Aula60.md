## Verbos
- To rain, To travel, To stay, To start, To visit

## Gramática
- May for permission; might for possibility.

## Preparação
- May I open _____
- It might not _____
- May we visit the _____

## Homework
1. May I open the window?
2. Might it rain this afternoon?
3. May we visit the museum tomorrow?
4. Do you use cloudy sky in your routine?
5. Can you make a sentence with travel plan?
