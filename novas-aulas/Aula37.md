## Verbos
- To be, To happen, To describe

## Gramática
- Was / were with weather and event descriptions.

## Preparação
- It was sunny _____
- The festival wasn't empty _____
- Were the streets wet after _____

## Homework
1. Was it sunny on Sunday?
2. Was the festival crowded at night?
3. Were the streets wet after the rain?
4. Do you use sunny beach in your routine?
5. Can you make a sentence with rainy afternoon?
