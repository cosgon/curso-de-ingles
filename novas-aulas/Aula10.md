- Practice line
- Exam folder

## Verbos
- To complete, To identify, To mark, To write, To check

## Gramática
- Leitura, escrita e perguntas curtas no Simple Present.

## Preparação
- I complete the _____
- The student doesn't write _____
- Does the teacher check the _____

## Homework
1. Do you complete the test calmly?
2. Does the student write short answers?
3. Does the teacher check the answer sheet?
4. Do you use answer sheet in your routine?
5. Can you make a sentence with pencil case?
