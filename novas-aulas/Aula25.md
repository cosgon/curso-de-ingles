## Verbos
- To have, To show, To describe

## Gramática
- There is / There are em descricoes de casa.

## Preparação
- There is a big sofa in the _____
- There are not five chairs in _____
- Is there a mirror on _____

## Homework
1. Is there a big sofa in the living room?
2. Are there two chairs in the kitchen?
3. Is there a mirror on the wall?
4. Do you use big sofa in your routine?
5. Can you make a sentence with wooden table?
